# Hidden Door

Create and explore interactive story worlds with [Hidden Door](https://www.hiddendoor.co/). Import your writing
and develop characters, settings, and storylines through conversation with Claude.
This plugin bundles the Hidden Door MCP connection with workflow skills.

## What you can do

- **Create a world:** start with an idea, discuss and revise its preview, and create a draft world.
- **Import your writing:** turn existing stories, setting notes, or manuscripts
  into a world draft.
- **Develop a world:** revise characters, locations, storylines, tone, and rules
  in an existing world.
- **Explore a world:** take a guided tour of its characters, playable roles, and
  storylines, and make changes as you go.
- **Get oriented:** ask questions about Hidden Door and choose a workflow.

## Try asking

- “Help me create a world where ghosts return one night a year.”
- “Import my draft worldbuilding notes into a Hidden Door world.”
- “Walk me through the characters and storylines in my world.”
- “Make this character more suspicious of strangers, but keep her backstory.”

## Setup

You need a Hidden Door account with access to the worlds you want to
work on. The first time the plugin connects, you will be prompted to
sign in to Hidden Door in your browser.

The plugin sends your writing, ideas, and requested edits to Hidden Door, and
saves drafts and changes to your Hidden Door account.
